# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the AethelGard project welcoming,
respectful, and free from harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, evidence, and project decisions.
- Protect private data and follow the repository's security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information,
retaliation, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's configured
contact channel. For security-sensitive matters, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead of opening a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as the
available tooling and circumstances allow.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, limit participation, or take other
appropriate action when this code is violated. They will explain decisions when doing
so without exposing private information.

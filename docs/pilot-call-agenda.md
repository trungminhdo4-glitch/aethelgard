# Pilot Call Agenda

## Thirty Minutes

| Time | Topic |
|---:|---|
| 5 min | Problem: how the team reviews security/NIS-2 evidence today |
| 5 min | Current document process, document types, and bottlenecks |
| 10 min | Demo with customer-like synthetic sample pack |
| 5 min | Data handling, exclusions, retention, and human review |
| 5 min | Next step: redacted non-sensitive sample pack and pilot gate |

## Discovery Questions

- Which documents consume the most first-pass review time?
- Which categories matter most: risk, incidents, suppliers, access control, continuity,
  or vulnerabilities?
- What must be excluded before any sample is shared?
- Who performs the human review today?
- What would make a 7-day pilot useful enough to continue?

## Go/No-Go

GO when:

- customer can provide 3 to 10 non-sensitive/redacted documents
- customer understands human review remains mandatory
- no legal, audit, certification, or compliance guarantee is expected
- data-handling boundaries are accepted
- clear company-side contact for the pilot exists

NO-GO when:

- customer wants a compliance guarantee
- customer wants to send sensitive incident/customer data
- customer requires SaaS or multi-tenant processing
- customer expects legal advice
- customer wants productive processing without legal/privacy review

## Close

The next step is only a redacted sample pack. No secrets, no production logs, no raw
incident data, and no avoidable personal data.

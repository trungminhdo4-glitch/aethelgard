# Berlin Outreach Target Research

## Executive Summary

This pack identifies 15 Berlin/Brandenburg outreach candidates for Aethelgard. The strongest first-contact pattern is not the largest security consultancy, but a pragmatic MSP / IT-Systemhaus with SME customers, recurring document handovers and a public contact path.

Aethelgard remains narrowly positioned:

```text
Aethelgard ist ein lokales Evidence-Triage-Tool fuer Security-/NIS2-Dokumente.
Es hilft bei der strukturierten Vorpruefung von Policies, ISMS-/Risk-/Incident-/Supplier-/Access-/BCM-/Vulnerability-Dokumenten.
Es ersetzt keine Rechtsberatung, kein Audit und keine Zertifizierung.
Human Review bleibt Pflicht.
```

## Owner Manual Checks Before Sending

- Confirm the website is still current on the day of sending.
- Confirm the contact channel is public and company-level.
- Do not use personal contacts, private inboxes or employee profile pages.
- Re-read the message for narrow Aethelgard claims.
- Confirm the message does not promise compliance, audit results, legal advice or certification.
- Use synthetic data or a redacted non-sensitive sample pack only.
- Check whether the company looks like a direct product/platform competitor.
- Record follow-up timing manually; do not automate sending.

# ICP Scoring

Use this matrix to choose the first 3 to 5 outreach targets. Score each factor from 1 to
5. Higher is better except `Trust Barrier` and `Risk`, where higher means harder or
riskier.

Score formula:

`Pain + Urgency + Access + Pilot Fit + Willingness to Pay - Trust Barrier - Risk`

| ICP | Pain | Urgency | Access | Trust Barrier | Pilot Fit | Willingness to Pay | Risk | Score |
| --- | ---: | ------: | -----: | ------------: | --------: | -----------------: | ---: | ----: |
| Small security-/compliance consultancy | 5 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 15 |
| MSP / IT-Systemhaus with SME customers | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 17 |
| NIS-2-affected SME directly | 4 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 7 |
| Data protection / ISMS consultancy | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 12 |
| Software agency with compliance customers | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 5 |

## Best First ICP

MSP / IT-Systemhaus with SME customers.

This group has repeated customer-side documentation work, understands operational
security constraints, and can test Aethelgard as a local review accelerator without
turning the pilot into a legal or audit engagement.

## Top Three Target Groups

1. MSP / IT-Systemhaus with SME customers
2. Small security-/compliance consultancy
3. Data protection / ISMS consultancy

## Lower Priority For First Wave

- NIS-2-affected SME directly: useful later, but higher trust barrier and more education
  effort for a first pilot.
- Software agency with compliance customers: may be adjacent, but the pain is less direct
  unless they already run security documentation projects.

## Selection Rule

Prefer targets that already mention managed IT, security consulting, ISMS, ISO 27001,
TISAX, BSI, NIS-2, supplier security, or compliance support on their public website.
Do not collect personal contact data. Use only company-level public contact channels.

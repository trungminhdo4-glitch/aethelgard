# Target Selection Guide

Use this guide to manually choose 3 to 5 first-wave targets. Do not scrape leads, do not
store personal contact details, and do not use LinkedIn automation or lead databases.

## Manual Workflow

1. Search only public company websites or known public directories manually.
2. Check whether the company-level offer fits one of the top ICPs.
3. Record only company-level information in `docs/outreach-target-list-template.csv`.
4. Use public company contact channels such as a contact form, website URL, or generic
   mailbox shown by the company.
5. Stop after 3 to 5 good-fit targets for the first wave.

## Relevant Signals

- MSP betreut KMU.
- Security, ISMS, or NIS-2 is mentioned.
- Compliance consulting is offered.
- Managed IT services are a core business.
- ISO 27001, TISAX, BSI, supplier security, or NIS-2 topics are visible.
- Clear public company contact channel exists.
- The company likely reviews policies, supplier questionnaires, or security procedures.

## Avoid These Targets

- Large enterprises for the first wave.
- Pure webdesign agencies without security/compliance focus.
- Companies without any security, ISMS, MSP, or compliance signal.
- Companies with only recruiting or career contact channels.
- Companies that appear to require sensitive regulated data before any discussion.
- Any target where the owner cannot explain the fit in one sentence.

## Fit Notes

Good `why_fit` examples:

- "MSP for SME customers; public NIS-2/security consulting page; likely recurring document
  review pain."
- "Small ISMS consultancy; mentions ISO 27001 and supplier questionnaires; local
  first-pass triage may reduce review sorting time."

Weak `why_fit` examples:

- "Looks like a tech company."
- "Might know someone."
- "Has compliance in a blog post but no service fit."

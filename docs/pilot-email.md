# AethelGard Pilot Email

## Variant 1: MSP / Security Consultancy

Subject: Local evidence triage for NIS-2 document reviews

Hi [Name],

your team probably spends a lot of time opening policy PDFs, supplier questionnaires,
and security procedures just to find whether usable evidence is present.

AethelGard is a local evidence-triage pilot for that first review pass. It processes a
small non-sensitive document pack, extracts bounded snippets, groups likely evidence by
category, and flags possible gaps for human review.

It is not legal advice, not a certification, and not an automated compliance decision.
The practical goal is simpler: help your consultants move faster before they apply their
own judgement.

Pilot scope:

- 3 to 10 non-sensitive documents
- local processing only
- JSON and Markdown report handover
- false-positive / false-negative review with your team
- 7-day or 14-day option

Would it be useful to test this on one redacted sample pack from a current NIS-2 or
supplier-security engagement?

## Variant 2: Small NIS-2-Affected Company

Subject: Faster first pass over your security documents

Hi [Name],

if you are preparing NIS-2 or supplier-security documentation, one painful step is often
finding which existing documents already contain useful evidence and where obvious gaps
remain.

secrets, logs, production data, or personal data for this pilot.

time before your team or advisor makes the final judgement?

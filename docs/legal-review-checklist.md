# Legal Review Checklist for Paid Pilot

Template for review, not legal advice. Must be reviewed by a qualified legal/privacy advisor
before using paid pilot documents with customers.

## Must Be Reviewed

- AVV/DPA need
- Controller/processor role
- Retention period
- Deletion confirmation wording
- Liability/disclaimer
- Customer data categories
- Subprocessors
- Cross-border transfers
- Handling of personal data in policies
- Incident-data exclusion
